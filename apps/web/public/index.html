ClassroomPilot

ClassroomPilot connects your Google Classroom to the AI assistant you already use. Your assistant can see what is due, read assignment instructions, rubrics, announcements and class materials, and help you prepare your work. You always review your work and press Turn In yourself.

How it works

  1. Add the ClassroomPilot connector URL to your AI assistant.
  2. Sign in with Google and approve read-only access to your Classroom.
  3. Ask your assistant about your coursework.

What it never does

  • It never changes, deletes or shares anything in your Classroom or Drive.
  • It never turns in work for you.
  • It never reads other students' work.
