Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What we access

When you sign in with Google, you grant ClassroomPilot read-only access to: your Google profile and email address; your Google Classroom courses, classwork, class materials, announcements and topics; and your own submissions and grades. We also read the content of Google Docs, Slides and Sheets files attached to your classwork, and, only when you approve a specific change in your AI assistant, we edit your own submission copy of a Google Doc.

How we use it

We use this data only to answer requests you make through your AI assistant, such as listing assignments, showing instructions and rubrics, and reading attached files, so that the assistant can help you with your coursework. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

What we store

  • A Google refresh token, stored encrypted and tied to your connection, so we can reach Classroom on your behalf.
  • Connection records needed to keep your AI assistant signed in.

Classroom content is fetched when you ask for it and passed to your AI assistant. We do not keep a copy of your coursework on our servers.

Your AI assistant

Data we return goes to the AI assistant you connected (for example ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini). That provider handles it under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not share your Google user data with anyone except: your connected AI assistant at your request; our hosting provider (Cloudflare), which processes data on our behalf; and when required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ClassroomPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which revokes our access. To have the stored connection records deleted, email privacy@raycode.tech; we will delete them within 30 days.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Stored tokens are encrypted. Content from Classroom is treated as untrusted and is labelled as data for your AI assistant.
